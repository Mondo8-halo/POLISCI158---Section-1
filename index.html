About me

I am a sophomore studying design at Stanford University. I recently moved from Colorado to San Luis Obispo, California, about three hours from campus.

After a summer internship where AI knowledge was in high demand, I wanted to learn more about it. I believe POLISCI 158 will be a good place to do that.

Research interests

  • How AI and human designers should interact What the working relationship between designers and AI tools should look like, and who stays in charge of the creative decisions.
  • Telling real from AI-generated Whether people can truly distinguish AI-generated content from what is real.
  • AI and human creativity Whether AI limits human creative capabilities or increases them.

Project ideas for POLISCI 158

  • Can people tell what is real? A small experiment that asks participants to tell AI-generated content apart from authentic content, then measures how accurately they can do it.
